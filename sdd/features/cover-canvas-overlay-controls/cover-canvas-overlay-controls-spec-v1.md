# Cover canvas overlay controls — spec v1

## Intent

Keep the cover artwork unobstructed by removing the floating zoom strip and the redundant canvas name/dimensions label.

## Requirements

1. Do not render zoom buttons or a zoom percentage over or beneath the canvas.
2. Keep zoom adjustment available through the zoom selector in the top workspace toolbar.
3. Do not render the «Lienzo de portada/contraportada» label or fixed-looking dimensions under the layers panel.
4. Retain canvas guide, view, alignment, and layer controls in the left workspace.
5. Apply the same behavior to cover and back cover.

## Acceptance criteria

- No floating canvas zoom control overlaps the design.
- The toolbar zoom selector remains available and functional.
- Canvas name/dimension text is absent in both editors.
