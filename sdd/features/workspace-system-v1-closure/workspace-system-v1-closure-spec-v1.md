## 3. Remediated Gaps Summary

1. **WS-01 (`CreateProjectForm.tsx` i18n parity)**: Added `newProjectProjectStepTitle`, `newProjectProjectStepDescription`, `newProjectSummaryEyebrow`, `newProjectSummaryDescription`, `newProjectSummaryTemplate`, and `newProjectSummaryReady` to `AppMessages['project']` (`es` and `en`) and replaced hardcoded Spanish strings in `CreateProjectForm.tsx`.
2. **WS-02 (`ProjectWorkspace.tsx` topbar & fallback rail i18n parity)**: Added `contentSaving` and `contentSaved` to `AppMessages['project']` (`es` and `en`) and localized the topbar save status and fallback fixed-PDF progress rail in `ProjectWorkspace.tsx`.
3. **WS-03 (`ProjectWorkspace.tsx` & `/projects/[projectId]/preview/page.tsx` step/modal query deep-linking)**: `ProjectWorkspace` now honors `?step=1..8` and `?documentData=open` from `window.location.search` on mount, and `/projects/[projectId]/preview` sets `exportHref` to `/projects/${project.id}/editor?step=8` so clicking `Exportar` from the standalone preview route lands directly on Step 8 (`Exportar`).
4. **WS-04 (`AdvancedCoverEditor.tsx` low-resolution warning i18n parity)**: Added `lowResImageWarning` and `lowResCoverWarning` to `AppMessages['coverDesignSurface']['workspace']` (`es` and `en`) and replaced hardcoded Spanish strings in `AdvancedCoverEditor.tsx`.

## 4. Acceptance Criteria

1. `messages-parity.test.ts` passes with 100% key symmetry and zero empty strings across `es` and `en`.
2. `CreateProjectForm` renders English copy for Step 1 heading/description and Summary eyebrow/description/template/ready state when given `resolveLocaleMessages('en').project`.
3. `ProjectWorkspace` opens directly on Step `N` when `?step=N` (`1..8`) is present in `window.location.search`, and opens `DocumentDataModal` when `?documentData=open` is present.
4. All affected component tests (`CreateProjectForm.test.tsx`, `ProjectWorkspace.test.tsx`, `CoverWorkspace08A.test.tsx`) pass without regression.
